

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing employee workforce data to identify HR trends, understand employee attrition, and generate meaningful business insights.

The project uses Microsoft Excel to organise employee data, perform KPI analysis, and present findings through an HR dashboard.

🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyse employee attrition patterns across departments.
* Understand workforce distribution and job roles.
* Evaluate employee salary and hourly rate metrics.
* Examine employee experience and total working years.
* Identify trends that can support HR decision-making.

📊 Dataset Overview

* Total Employee Records: 50,000
* Tool Used: Microsoft Excel
* Data Format: Excel Workbook (.xlsx)

Key Data Attributes

* Employee demographics
* Department and job role
* Business travel
* Monthly income and hourly rate
* Employee attrition
* Job satisfaction and performance
* Total working years
* Work-life balance
* Training and experience

📈 Project Components

1. HR Dashboard

A dedicated dashboard sheet for presenting HR analytics visually.

2. KPI Analysis

Six KPI worksheets containing summary and pivot-based analysis.

Key analysis areas include:

* Attrition by department
* Average hourly rate
* Employee distribution
* Average working years by department
* Job-role comparisons

3. Data Worksheets

* HR_Combined
* HR_1
* HR_2

These worksheets contain employee-level information used in the analysis.

🛠️ Tools & Techniques

* Microsoft Excel
* Data Cleaning and Preparation
* PivotTables
* KPI Analysis
* Dashboard Reporting
* Data Analysis and Interpretation

💡 Business Value

This analysis helps HR teams understand workforce patterns, monitor employee attrition, evaluate employee-related metrics, and support data-driven workforce planning.
